Rules:
- White only, always on the dark background. Don't recolor, add gradients, outlines or shadows to the logo itself.
- Header height: `h-7` mobile / `h-8` desktop. Mark in footer: `h-12`.
- The **orbit** in the mark is the brand's visual motif — reuse it as thin rings (see 07), not by redrawing the logo.

## Voice

Friendly, plain-spoken, confident. A small-business partner, not a faceless agency.

- **Short sentences. Active voice.** "We build sites your team can actually update." Not "Solutions are delivered…"
- **Concrete over hype.** Say what happens ("Updates, security and backups"), not "synergistic digital transformation".
- **Space metaphors are seasoning, not the meal.** One per section at most: "out of this *world*", "on the *horizon*", "Lost in space." Never stack them.
- **Benefits for a business owner.** Customers, calls, leads, speed, peace of mind.
- **No invented proof.** No fake testimonials, client logos, stats or case studies. Stats on the site come from the live site (`STATS`). Projects and posts are entered by the team in `/admin`.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons ("Start a project", not "Start A Project"). Eyebrows are uppercase via CSS — write them in sentence case.
- **Numbers:** "250+", "100%", "$2,000 – $5,000" (en dash with spaces for ranges), "Mon–Fri" (en dash, no spaces for short ranges).
- **Punctuation:** curly quotes “ ” in copy, em dashes — with spaces, `&apos;` in JSX.

## Headline formula

Two short lines, the last word(s) in `<Accent>`:

- "Elevate your business with customized web *solutions.*"
- "Build it. Keep it healthy. Bring people *to it.*"
- "Work that's live — and work on the *horizon.*"
- "Notes from the *studio.*"
